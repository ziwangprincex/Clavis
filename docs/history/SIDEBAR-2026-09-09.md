# Sidebar correction · 2026-09-09

The owner rejected the visible Workspace heading and the Document/underline
navigation. They also questioned why Research had disappeared. This pass
corrects the local product-flow candidate; it is not a release or a new theme.

## Changes

- Remove the visible Workspace heading and underlined text tabs. Use compact
  32px icon buttons with a theme-owned selected background/outline. Document
  and Research remain available; Project still depends on build/Git tools.
  Icons have localized tooltips, accessible tab names, panel relationships,
  roving focus, arrow/Home/End navigation. Workspace remains an accessibility
  label only, not visible copy.
- Research no longer disappears without a folder. Keep the selected Research
  view when its data goes away, with a short explanation and an Open folder
  action. Bibliography/references/assets retain their existing format rules.
- The folder component owns its header; remove the extra Folder wrapper.
  Without a folder, show one Open folder row instead of two empty labels and
  a rescan button. An open folder still shows its real name and existing actions.
- Outline owns its collapsible section and renders nothing without headings.
  Existing Markdown/LaTeX/Typst parsing and multi-file jumps are unchanged.
  Empty/whitespace-only drafts do not expose Writing checks; nonempty documents
  retain the collapsed section and existing commands.
- Tighten section spacing and place disclosure chevrons before labels. No new
  font, palette, panel, editor inset, document mutation, or user-setting change.
